**Date:** 2026-10-13

## Core finding

Two distinct texts must not be conflated: **Wizards of the Coast, The Clone Wars Campaign Guide (2009)** is the official game book; **Clone Wars Saga Edition Fan Sourcebook** is explicitly indexed by SWSE Fandom as a community **Homebrew** work. Era association, an NPC label, or a planet's sapient-species description does **not** convert the fan sourcebook's race statistics into official published SWSE racial mechanics.

Original source inspection used the uploaded **225-page scanned** `SAGA EDITION - Clone Wars Campaign Guide.pdf` and full `Clone Wars Campaign Guide_djvu.txt` searchable text. Physical PDF pages 10–18 were rendered and inspected as the main Chapter I species section, with targeted Chagrian pages near physical 160–161 also checked and remaining NPC, gazetteer and organization names searched. The main official chapter presents Dug, Gen'Dai, Iktotchi, Kaleesh, Kaminoan, Kerkoiden, Nautolan, Nelvaanian and Vurk; Republic Clone appears elsewhere and Chagrian has a later racial block. **No standalone playable race-traits block was found for any of the 17 marked-for-removal candidates.**

## Direct wiki publication distinction

- [Official Clone Wars Campaign Guide species chapter index](https://swse.fandom.com/wiki/CWCG_Species) — community transcription of official rulebook chapter, distinct from fan options.
- [Clone Wars Saga Edition Fan Sourcebook species list](https://swse.fandom.com/wiki/CWSB_Species) — explicitly labeled `Homebrew Reference Book` and `Untested`; identifies **Koorivar**, **Northern/Southern Mustafarian**, **Muun**, and **Ortolan** with playable conversion modifiers.
- [Koorivar entry](https://swse.fandom.com/wiki/Koorivar) — explicitly attributes player rules to Clone Wars *Fan Sourcebook*, not the printed WotC book.
- [Mirialan entry](https://swse.fandom.com/wiki/Mirialan) — expressly credits named community authors for species mechanics, independent of published characters/near-Human appearances.
- [Quinlan Vos official character entry](https://swse.fandom.com/wiki/Quinlan_Vos) — printed `Kiffar (Near-Human)` NPC does not supply a standalone Kiffar racial adjustment block.

## Geonosian indexing discrepancy — not part of removal list

SWSE Fandom's [CWCG species index](https://swse.fandom.com/wiki/CWCG_Species) lists Geonosian alongside Chapter I options, while the uploaded official PDF Chapter I's complete printed racial-block sequence does **not** include Geonosian. The scanned book DOES include Geonosian lore and warrior NPC statistics. An earlier Wizards *Saga Edition Conversion Guide* has Geonosian conversion rules, and the Fandom species page attributes a main variant to CWCG. Because these sources do not align cleanly, the previously recorded **Geonosian source/version conflict remains OPEN**; this does not place Geonosian in the homebrew removal queue.

## Certification outcome and safety policy

1. **Removal queue unchanged: 17 records marked, zero removed.** The check did not uncover published WotC Clone Wars racial-stat authority for any of the 17 playable implementations currently marked for removal.
2. The confirmed fan-book species should not be identified as WotC content merely because the fan-sourcebook title contains `Clone Wars`.
3. Fandom source labels are research/navigation evidence, not a substitute for the original copyright-bearing species mechanics. A future legitimate official racial source can override an individual removal candidate.
4. Existing world actors, species IDs, registries, trait sidecars and V2 CharGen remain intact pending the approved controlled-removal stage.
5. The recorded 121 source-audited identities and 302 historical findings are unchanged. This recheck adds research evidence, not production fixes.

**Result:** No basis from the original Clone Wars hardcover to rescind any of the 17 removal markers. Reassess elsewhere if a separate official WotC printed/web racial species source is recovered.
