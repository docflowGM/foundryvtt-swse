Classes: CANONICAL_AUTHORITY, CANONICAL_DELEGATE, READ_ONLY_CONSUMER, CONTEXTUAL_CONTRIBUTION, DUPLICATE_FORMULA, COMPATIBILITY_DEBT, UNREACHABLE, UNVERIFIED.
