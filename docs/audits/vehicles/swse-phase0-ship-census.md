## Rolling review notes

- Census is a rolling discovery list, not canon-certified, and not an exhaustive verified PDF page-by-page statblock audit.
- Some original TXT OCR interleaves two-column material, separates CL from a vessel name, or garbles letters/digits. The census includes manually recovered headings.
- Scum and Villainy TXT/OCR is substantially degraded. Its ship headings were additionally spot-checked against rendered PDF pages 63-68 and 127-132; full-book completeness review remains pending.
- Listed names follow printed statblock headings when available; normalization of manufacturer prefixes, model aliases, reprints and variants belongs in Phase 1.
- No compendium/database scraping was used. No GitHub or production files were modified.
- Not every vehicle is a ship: ground speeders, walkers, tanks and airspeeders not classed as spacecraft are excluded from this ship-only census.
- Phase 0-B schema source sweep recovered CEC VCX-350 Light Freighter from Threats of the Galaxy; prior Threats count of zero was incorrect. The DP-2 Probe Droid in Scavenger's Guide to Droids remains classified separately as a hybrid starfighter/ground-vehicle and is not added to the ordinary ship tally. Sourcebooks remain provisional.
- Phase 1 Clone Wars printed-PDF check: added the Diamond-class Cruiser (CL 13, printed p206); normalized Droch-class Boarding Ship from OCR Oroch; preserved the separately statblocked detachable Seltiss-2 Caravel Cabin (printed p81). Clone Wars 40->41; total 312->313. Phase 0 remains a rolling, not exhaustive, census.
- Phase 1 final-book review: Unknown Regions Drexi corrected to printed Drexl-class; Galaxy of Intrigue Ferret and Prowler independent CL12 ships added at pp69 and 70. Overall 314->316.
- Phase 1 ship census is a list of enumerated printed ship statblocks; zero counts for Scavenger's Guide do not certify absence of droid-vehicles or ground vehicle rules.
