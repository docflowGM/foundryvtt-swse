## Blockers

- **CONFIGURATION_NOT_IMPLEMENTED** (ENGINEERING): The configuration/modification row is SOURCE_VERIFIED only: no certified effect, no Shipyard installation, no attack-time behaviour.
- **LAUNCHER_AND_AMMUNITION_NOT_INSTALLABLE** (ENGINEERING): This launcher or its ammunition has no certified effect: the Shipyard cannot sell or install it. (Installable launchers use the shared finite-ammunition ledger and lock-on projectile engine, P6C-3C/3D.)
- **NO_COMPLETE_STOCK_LAUNCHER_FAMILY** (DATA): No published firing group carries a verified single launcher, so no stock launcher group is armed; 18 groups wait for a GM-certified launcher allocation.
- **SOURCE_ROW_NOT_RECONCILED** (SOURCE): The catalog row has no printed table row: its numbers came from vehicle statblocks. It cannot be priced or installed until a printed source is reconciled.
- **STATBLOCK_EQUIPMENT_ONLY** (SOURCE): Appears only inside vehicle statblocks (no weapon-systems entry in a sourcebook): not a purchasable component.
- **ION_BOMB_NOT_IMPLEMENTED** (RULES_DEFERRED): Register V-P6C-16 is awaiting an owner ruling (range and target of a dropped bomb). The shared launcher ledger, ion damage and area machinery are ready; ion bomb groups stay refused.
- **POINT_DEFENSE_NOT_IMPLEMENTED** (ENGINEERING): The point-defense modification is not implemented for this weapon.
- **POINT_DEFENSE_STOCK_CLASSIFICATION_UNVERIFIED** (SOURCE): 60 stock groups whose label says point-defense are refused: a statblock label is not evidence of the modification's rules (the gate never infers a mechanic from a name). The aftermarket option is implemented.
- **SPECIAL_WEAPON_NOT_INDIVIDUALLY_VERIFIED** (ENGINEERING): Source-specific mechanics need individual verification and implementation.
- **CAPACITY_EXPANSION_DEFERRED** (RULES_DEFERRED): Extra launcher capacity (+20% / +25% cost per additional unit up to the printed maximum) is recorded but not usable: whether added capacity costs emplacement points and which cost the percentage applies to is unresolved. Register V-P6A-03.
- **RELOAD_ACTION_UNRULED** (RULES_DEFERRED): Bought ammunition arrives in the launcher's reserve; moving it into the launcher is a GM operation (no reload action or cost is invented from personal launchers). Register V-P6C-06.
- **STOCK_BATTERY_FACTS_INCOMPLETE** (DATA): Most printed weapon batteries lack a printed weapon count. A GM can certify the count of a printed battery (guarded world-Actor configuration; never inferred from gunners); named-gunner rosters (P6B-D-071) and the stacking of several assisting batteries are not implemented.
- **OPTIONAL_LINK_NOT_IMPLEMENTED** (ENGINEERING): Swift-action link toggle (extra damage and ammunition use apply only while linked) is not implemented.
- **FIRE_LINKED_LAUNCHERS_BUCKETED** (RULES_DEFERRED): Fire-linked LAUNCHERS (one shot per linked launcher, launcher count and magazine of a linked pair) and the optional fire link (a swift-action toggle on a fire-linked weapon) are not implemented: the published text gives no launcher count or capacity for a linked launcher. Register V-P6C-06; bucketed as P6B-D-050 remainder; fire-linked energy weapons are implemented.
- **TRACTOR_BEAM_RESIDUALS_OPEN** (ENGINEERING): Stock and aftermarket tractor beams run through the shared weapon-control / grapple engines. Not implemented: a target larger than the beam's vehicle, docking clamps.
- **HYPERDRIVE_ACTIVATION_HAS_NO_CONSUMER** (ENGINEERING): The 4x4 field, the attack against Reflex 10, the persistent scene record and the per-turn interdiction effect are implemented, and the sheet shows the interdiction; the system has NO hyperdrive activation action, so nothing yet REFUSES a jump (isHyperdriveInterdicted is the query that action must consult). Fields last until a GM ends them (V-P6C-14).
- **MINE_NOTICE_RULING_PENDING** (RULES_DEFERRED): Register V-P6C-15 is awaiting an owner ruling: how the Stealth +5 forms the DC for the pilot / system-operator reaction to notice a mine.
- **ADVANCED_MINE_NOT_OFFERED** (DATA): The advanced mine (+10, 9d10x2) has no launcher row in the catalog and is not offered.
- **MINE_PATH_CROSSING_NOT_MODELED** (ENGINEERING): Only the destination footprint of a moving token is tested against a mine square; squares crossed during a move are not.
- **GRAVITY_MINE_NOT_IMPLEMENTED** (ENGINEERING): The Legacy Era Campaign Guide gravity mines (standard area, magnetic hull-attached) are a different source and mechanic from the SotG mines and gravity wells; not implemented.
- **NO_CERTIFIED_EFFECT** (SOURCE): The catalog row is SOURCE_VERIFIED only: no certified weapon specification, so it cannot be installed.

## Established source facts, by family

### Laser cannons: light, medium, heavy

Sources: Starships of the Galaxy pp. 44-47 - Weapon Systems: Laser Cannons; Starships of the Galaxy pp. 47 - Table 3-5: Starship Weapons; Core Rulebook - Vehicles, Table 10-2: Vehicle Weapon Ranges

- Table 3-5: 3d10x2 / 4d10x2 / 5d10x2; 1 / 1 / 2 EP; Licensed / Restricted / Military; 1,500 / 4,000 / 6,000 credits.
- Laser cannons are the most common starship weapons, carried by starfighters and transports as primary armament and by capital ships as point-defense weapons; better range than blaster cannons, somewhat more expensive.
- Damage = weapon dice x printed multiplier (Core, Vehicle Combat: Damage).

### Blaster cannons: light, medium, heavy

Sources: Starships of the Galaxy pp. 44-47 - Weapon Systems: Blaster Cannons; Starships of the Galaxy pp. 47 - Table 3-5: Starship Weapons; Core Rulebook - Vehicles, Table 10-2: Vehicle Weapon Ranges

- Table 3-5: 3d10x2 / 4d10x2 / 5d10x2; 1 / 1 / 2 EP; Licensed / Restricted / Military; 1,200 / 2,500 / 5,500 credits.
- Blasters are less expensive than lasers but have a more limited range (Table 10-2: shorter bands than laser cannons).

### Autoblaster (modification of a single-mounted blaster cannon)

Sources: Starships of the Galaxy pp. 44-47 - Weapon Systems: Autoblaster; Starships of the Galaxy pp. 47 - Table 3-5: Starship Weapons

- Table 3-5: no damage printed; +0 EP; Restricted; cost x2 (a TOTAL-cost multiplier, V-P6C-11).
- Blaster cannons can be designed with a higher cyclic firing rate if they have sufficient cooling; only single-mounted blasters are generally made into autoblasters.
- Owner interpretation V-P6C-09: the autoblaster grants autofire capability to an eligible single-mounted blaster cannon; the base dice are unchanged; normal fire and autofire both remain; no compatibility with double or quad is inferred.

### Turbolasers: light, medium, heavy

Sources: Starships of the Galaxy pp. 44-47 - Weapon Systems: Turbolasers; Starships of the Galaxy pp. 47 - Table 3-5: Starship Weapons; Core Rulebook - Vehicles, Table 10-2: Vehicle Weapon Ranges

- Table 3-5: 3d10x5 / 5d10x5 / 7d10x5; 2 / 5 / 10 EP; Military; 5,000 / 10,000 / 20,000 credits; Colossal or larger starships only.
- All but the smallest turbolasers cannot autofire even in double, quad or fire-linked configurations.
- Capital weapons take the Core -20 against targets smaller than Colossal (existing capital-ship penalty authority).

### Ion cannons: light, medium, heavy

Sources: Starships of the Galaxy pp. 44-47 - Weapon Systems: Ion Cannons; Starships of the Galaxy pp. 47 - Table 3-5: Starship Weapons; Core Rulebook - Vehicles, Table 10-2: Vehicle Weapon Ranges; Core Rulebook - Damage: Ion damage, pp. 159-160

- Table 3-5: 3d10x2 / 5d10x2 / 3d10x5 ion; 1 / 2 / 5 EP; Licensed / Restricted / Military; 2,000 / 3,000 / 6,000 credits; heavy: Colossal or larger only.
- Ion cannons deal ion damage (Core ch. 9). All but the smallest cannot autofire, even in double, quad or fire-linked configurations.
- Ion damage lifecycle on the existing damage authorities: V-P6C-04/05 (project interpretation, decision matrix).

### Hapan triple ion cannon

Sources: Starships of the Galaxy pp. 44-47 - Weapon Systems: Ion Cannons (Hapan triple); Starships of the Galaxy pp. 47 - Table 3-5: Starship Weapons

- Table 3-5: 5d10x5 ion; 3 EP; Illegal; 12,000 credits; Colossal or larger only.
- Acts like a point-defense weapon (point-defense ranges, no penalty against ships smaller than Colossal), is capable of autofire mode, may not make starship-scale area attacks. Illegal outside the Hapan Consortium until the New Jedi Order era; the Hapans do not sell them to outsiders.

### Mass-driver cannons and rail cannons

Sources: Clone Wars Campaign Guide - Republic Vehicles: Weapon Systems (Mass-Driver Cannon, Rail Cannon)

- Mass-driver and rail cannons can only be mounted on vehicles of Gargantuan size or larger because of their recoil.
- No table row prints their damage, EP or cost: the catalog numbers come from vehicle statblocks.

### Proton torpedoes and launcher

Sources: Starships of the Galaxy pp. 44-47 - Weapon Systems: Proton Torpedoes; Starships of the Galaxy pp. 47 - Table 3-5: Starship Weapons; Core Rulebook - Missiles and Torpedoes

- Table 3-5: torpedo 9d10x2, 0 EP, Military, 800 credits; launcher 1 EP, Military, 2,500 credits.
- 4-square splash radius. A standard launcher holds three torpedoes; additional capacity costs +25% per additional torpedo up to sixteen.
- Aimed launch that misses: one follow-up next turn at the same bonus -5, no action; Reflex Defense 30, 10 hit points; second miss self-destructs (Core).

### Concussion missiles and launchers: light, medium, heavy

Sources: Starships of the Galaxy pp. 44-47 - Weapon Systems: Concussion Missiles; Starships of the Galaxy pp. 47 - Table 3-5: Starship Weapons; Core Rulebook - Missiles and Torpedoes

- Table 3-5: missiles 7d10x2 / 9d10x2 / 9d10x5 (heavy: Colossal or larger), 500 / 750 / 2,000 credits; launchers 2 / 5 / 20 EP, 2,000 / 3,500 / 30,000 credits.
- 4-square splash radius. Launcher capacity: light 6, medium 16, heavy 30; additional capacity +20% per missile up to double.

### Armor-piercing concussion missiles

Sources: Starships of the Galaxy pp. 44-47 - Weapon Systems: Concussion Missiles (Armor-Piercing)

- Penetration 10 (ignores the first 10 points of DR or SR) at a -5 penalty on attack rolls with the weapon.
- No catalog row exists (recorded as a missing canonical component, sotg-concussion-missile-armor-piercing).

### Shieldbuster torpedoes and launcher

Sources: Starships of the Galaxy pp. 44-47 - Weapon Systems: Shieldbuster Torpedoes; Starships of the Galaxy pp. 47 - Table 3-5: Starship Weapons

- Table 3-5: torpedo 10d10x2, 0 EP, Military, 1,000 credits; launcher 3 EP, Military, 10,000 credits; Table 3-5 prints its own range row (character scale long band 2,251-4,000; starship 0-3 / 4-6 / 7-15 / 16-30).
- Against anything but shields, shieldbusters deal half damage. If an attack exceeds the target's SR, the target loses 10 points of SR (not the normal 5); shields recharge normally.
- A standard launcher holds four torpedoes, up to eight at +25% per additional torpedo.

### Space mines and mine launchers

Sources: Starships of the Galaxy pp. 44-47 - Weapon Systems: Space Mines; Starships of the Galaxy pp. 47 - Table 3-5: Starship Weapons

- Table 3-5: mines 7d10x2 / 9d10x2 / 6d10x5 (heavy takes the -20 against targets smaller than Colossal), launcher 2 EP 5,000 credits, heavy launcher 4 EP (Colossal or larger).

### Tractor beams

Sources: Starships of the Galaxy pp. 44-47 - Weapon Systems: Tractor Beams; Starships of the Galaxy pp. 47 - Table 3-5: Starship Weapons; Core Rulebook - Vehicles: Tractor Beams (SotG cites p. 174)

- Table 3-5: Special; 10 EP; Licensed; 10,000 credits. Considered a nonstandard modification for space transports and starfighters.
- Core: instead of damaging hit points, a tractor beam prevents a vehicle from escaping. The attack hits on equal or exceeding Reflex; on a hit, make an opposed grapple check; a win grabs the target.
- A grabbed target of the beam vehicle's size or smaller cannot move and loses its Dexterity bonus to Reflex; each round another opposed grapple check; a win pulls the target up to 10 squares (1 at starship scale) or holds it; a loss frees it; leaving range breaks the hold.

### Point-defense weapon modification (a purchase option of an eligible weapon)

### Autofire mode

Sources: Starships of the Galaxy pp. 44-47 - Weapon Systems: Cannon, Double/Quad; Fire-Linked Weapons (Autofire); Core Rulebook - Special Combat Rules: Autofire; Vehicle Combat: Scale

- Double, quad and fire-linked blaster cannons, laser cannons, light ion cannons and light turbolasers can fire normally or in autofire mode; a swift action switches between them. Larger ion cannons and turbolasers cannot autofire.
- Core autofire: an area attack on a 2x2 area, one attack roll at -5 against every creature's Reflex; hit full damage, miss half; consumes ten shots.
- Core vehicle combat: if a vehicle weapon is capable of autofire it can make an area attack at character scale; at starship scale area attacks (including autofire) resolve as attacks on a single target.

### Burst Fire (feat) on vehicle weapons

Sources: Core Rulebook - Feats: Burst Fire; Special Combat Rules: Autofire

- Burst Fire is a feat: autofire against a specific creature instead of an area at -5 attack for +2 dice of damage, using five shots; not an area attack.
