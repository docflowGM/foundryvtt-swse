## 1. The WP4 correction
`InventoryEngine.setWeaponReadied(true)` returns `NO_CHANGE` only when READIED is **verified** (effective posture READIED, no pending identity, no gates). A stored EQUIPPED weapon falls through to the coordinator and returns `DEFERRED_RULES_REVIEW` (EQ-001) with no write, journal entry or spend; a declared READIED that the canonical resolvers do not establish returns `READINESS_NOT_VERIFIED`. Nothing infers appendages or grip. Regression tests: WP4 suite check 14, WP5 checks 1–2.

## 2. One shared consumer interface — `scripts/engine/equipment/combat-readiness.js`
Owns no rule and no state; composes per call: `resolveActorSpeciesProfile` (new **synchronous** live read of the SpeciesRegistry authority; fails closed when the registry is uninitialized, the species is unrecorded/unknown or the publication selection is ambiguous) → `readActorEquipmentState` (record + grip choice → anatomy → species permissions → occupancy → canonical grip → effective posture, plus pending-transaction, incomplete-occupancy, uncertain-collision and species-evidence gates) → `isItemActivated` (independent). It distinguishes declared posture, effective verified posture, occupancy, grip, activation, identity/pending and gates; `ready = effective READIED ∧ ¬pending ∧ no gates`. Exports: `readCombatReadiness`, `describeWeaponReadiness`, `getWeaponReadiness`, `isWeaponReadied`, `isWeaponHeld`, `getReadiedWeapons`, `listWeaponReadiness`, `isVirtualUnarmedAttack`, `isCombatSourceReady`.

**Sync-boundary answer:** no cache, no private fallback, no species from names, no provisional anatomy, no legacy `system.equipped` fallback. Every call re-reads the live actor and registry, so a result cannot outlive a species, anatomy, record, grip or configuration change (`ownedConfigurationSelection` feeds the shape, so a configuration change re-verifies the grip). Actors prepared before registry initialization fail closed and are re-prepared once it finishes (`system-init-hooks.js`). The reader gained gate `species-evidence-unavailable` (biological actors without a profile never receive a positive posture from the resolver's provisional default arms).

## 4. FireStateStore convergence and combined Draw & Ignite
* `setWielding` removed; `planWielding(weapon, hands)` is non-writing and returns the field patch the coordinator writes. `commitFired` no longer records a grip. `writeState` is field-scoped (`fireStateUpdateFor` writes only changed keys as dotted paths) and **never** writes `COORDINATOR_OWNED_FIRE_STATE_KEYS` (`wielded`), so an unrelated update cannot overwrite a newer coordinator-owned grip. `heldGroupOf`/`heldTogetherOptions` fail closed (EQ-002).
* **Combined Draw & Ignite** is a new coordinator intent `ready-ignite` (`InventoryEngine.drawAndActivateLightsaber`). Eligibility and cost come from the existing combined-action authority (the Quick Draw action-speed mutation's `drawAndIgniteLightsaber` combined effect; its feat prerequisite is filtered by that authority), exactly one Swift; the weapon must be a STOWED lightsaber that is not already lit; appendages and grip are explicit (otherwise `CHOICE_REQUIRED`). It is one journaled transaction: plan → confirm → PREPARED → one field-scoped Item write of posture + occupancy + grip + activation → verify → APPLIED → one spend → FINALIZED; usability is withheld until FINALIZED (the journal gate), a failed spend compensates field by field (no readiness, no ignition), and activation remains distinct from posture. **Owned-write boundary expansion (documented):** `IGNITE_PATHS` = `system.activated`, `system.active` (only where the Item already carries it), `flags.foundryvtt-swse.emitLight`, `flags.foundryvtt-swse.bladeColor` — exactly what `InventoryEngine.setActivated` writes when it ignites a lightsaber — allowed **only** for the `ready-ignite` intent; they must travel in the same transaction because igniting a drawn blade and drawing it are one action, and two independently committed transactions could leave a free ignition or a double spend. The token-light sync stays a consumer and runs after a committed outcome. Deactivate-before-stow is unchanged.
