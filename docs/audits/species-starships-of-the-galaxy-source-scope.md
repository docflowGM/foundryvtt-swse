## Species mentions deliberately NOT turned into racial grants

- **Geonosian Nantex starfighter, printed p.86 / PDF p.87:** specialized pheromonal avionics and flight-computer interface make this craft suited to Geonosian pilots and unsuitable for other species. The `+1 equipment` bonus on the ship's laser attack within 2 squares is a **starship capability**, not a Geonosian racial attack bonus.
- **Ginivex-class starfighter, printed p.87 / PDF p.88:** contrary to the Nantex, this ship is designed for human pilots. This does not add a Human racial feat or skill.
- **Mon Calamari MC80 Star Cruiser, printed pp.114–115:** the description of Mon Calamari bridge crew and control design is ship-specific, not an automatic Mon Calamari racial proficiency.

No row was added to an individual species merely because that species is named in the ship codex. A separate ship/vehicle compatibility audit could use these references, but that is not this species audit.
