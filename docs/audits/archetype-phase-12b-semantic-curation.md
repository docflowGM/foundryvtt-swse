Semantic QA rulings
- Squad Leader narrows Field Commander into small-unit coordination: direct ally support and teamwork stay primary, while broad tactics is supporting.
- Tactical Droid is not merely a generic commander with a droid label: machine identity and tactical coordination are primary; recovery/resilience from Maintain Focus are reinforcing rather than defining.
- Droid Commander promotes droid, command, leadership, and ally support; Expanded Sensors supports sensors/detection/network without turning the archetype into a generic slicer.
- Mandalorian Armorer promotes armor/crafting/equipment and technical modification because the narrative authority explicitly defines a forge-keeper identity; this is stronger evidence than ordinary Mandalorian visual association.
- Geonosian Arena Champion uses Personal Vendetta as social battlefield control and protection-by-taunt; species identity alone does not generate unrelated insectoid tags.
- Zeison Sha Warrior promotes telekinesis and ranged discblade fighting; Force identity is intrinsic, but Jedi/lightsaber assumptions are not imported.
- Matukai Adept promotes martial arts, endurance, and resilience because its tradition is bodily Force discipline; generic lightsaber identity is excluded.
- Shaper of Kro Var promotes nature and Force offense for elemental shaping; no spellcasting tag is used because the published mechanics are Force powers, not a separate spell system.
- Jensaarai Defender promotes armor/defense/Force defense and keeps stealth/infiltration supporting from Force Cloak; dark_side is not inferred from mixed Jedi/Sith historical origins.
- Iron Knight promotes droid identity from the Shard-in-droid-body authority and recovery from Force Repair; Silicon Mind supports anti-Force/Force-defense semantics without making anti-Force the archetype core.
- Kilian Ranger promotes shields, defense, and lightsaber-polearm identity from Shield Gauntlet Defense and Siang Lance Mastery; generic vehicle/ride tags are not promoted merely from ranger flavor.
- Jedi Guardian, Consular, and Sentinel are differentiated by martial protection, advisory/support knowledge, and practical investigation/technical problem solving rather than receiving identical generic Jedi tag sets.
- Jedi Peacekeeper is tagged for control/social de-escalation rather than generic melee dominance; lightsaber combat remains supporting.
- Jedi Artisan receives crafting from explicit narrative and Jedi Artisan talent-tree authority concerning lightsaber construction, not from the name alone.
- Jedi Seer and Dark Prophet both use precognition/visions, but Dark Prophet adds dark-side fear/manipulation while Jedi Seer stays an information/foresight identity.
- Jedi Shadow, Watchman, Investigator, Archaeologist, Researcher, and Explorer are kept distinct through infiltration, tracking, investigation, exploration, and science/knowledge evidence rather than generic Sentinel inheritance.
- Sith Sorcerer, Alchemist, Inquisitor, Scholar, Manipulator, Warrior, Assassin, and Marauder retain distinct semantic centers; Sith Alchemy does not automatically make every Sith record an alchemist.
- Imperial Inquisitor and Sith Inquisitor are deliberately separated: Imperial Inquisitor centers control/fear/manipulation as an institutional hunter-interrogator, while Sith Inquisitor promotes infiltration and investigation with alchemy/talisman as reinforcing Sith tools.
- Sith Marauder promotes damage and sustained offensive pressure beyond the broader Sith Warrior martial identity; this distinction is supported by its battle-first narrative and Multiattack package rather than invented solely for uniqueness.
- ExplorCorps Seeker promotes galactic lore, piloting, and space exploration because its authority is explicitly survey-ship and hyperspace-route oriented; Jedi Explorer remains the broader field-discovery identity.
- Weaponsmith promotes weapon empowerment because tuning and improving weapons is the archetype core; this narrows the broader crafter/inventor overlap without importing generic combat tags.
- MedCorps Healer and Jedi Healer retain substantial legitimate overlap, but MedCorps keeps medical/medicine/Treat Injury as core clinical-service semantics while Jedi Healer centers Force healing and Force support.
- Imperial Knight promotes armor, defense, lightsaber melee, and resilience from Knight armor/resolve mechanics; dark_side is not inferred from Imperial allegiance.
