## Step B → C: 12 talent-domain contaminants (413 → 401)

`scripts/data/feat-domain-guard.js` (present on `main` before this investigation started) declares `TALENT_ONLY_FEAT_CONTAMINANTS`, a 13-name deny list with this rationale in its own header comment:

> "SWSE talents that had been scraped/migrated into `data/feat-catalog.json` and the canonical feats compendium source as fake feat records."

Every one of these 13 names has a real, mechanically-distinct talent record in `packs/talents.db`. The "feat" version of each (recoverable only from the orphaned `packs/feat-catalog.db`) has different flavor text and different numbers than the talent — e.g. the "Weapon Specialization" feat record grants a flat "+2 bonus on damage rolls with the selected weapon group" while the real "Weapon Specialization" talent (Scoundrel/Soldier-adjacent) grants "+2 bonus on damage rolls with such weapons" *and* stacks with vehicle weapon attacks when the Heavy Weapons group is chosen, with materially different prerequisite/eligibility framing. This is consistent with `feat-domain-guard.js`'s claim that the feat-side text was fabricated during a scrape rather than copied from a real Core Rulebook feat entry.

**Correction note:** an earlier pass in this same investigation (2026-08-06) misread `packs/feat-catalog.db` as a *recovery* source and merged these 12 names back into `data/feat-catalog.json` (401 → 413), before `feat-domain-guard.js` was found and cross-checked. That merge was reverted the same day once the guard's deny list and the talent-pack duplication were discovered. `data/feat-validity-registry.json` now records all 13 names with status `talent_domain_not_feat`, citing `scripts/data/feat-domain-guard.js` as the authority. **Do not use `packs/feat-catalog.db` as a source of "missing" feats again** — it is pre-cleanup scaffolding, not a backup.
