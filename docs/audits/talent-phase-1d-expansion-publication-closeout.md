# PHASE 1D - Later Publication Expansion Closeout

**Status:** COMPLETE  
**Date:** 2026-09-26  
**Branch:** `audit/talent-phase-1-tree-census`

## Scope

This pass completes the Phase 1D publication layer: later sourcebooks adding talents to already-existing talent trees.

The registry model deliberately records these as publication edges rather than creating new tree identities.

```text
existing canonical tree
  -> later sourcebook publishes additional member(s)
  -> talentPublications[]
  -> aggregateCanonicalTalentNames
```

No production talent-pack changes are made here.

# Structural observations

The publication sweep confirms that later-book additions are a major part of SWSE talent-tree structure. Treating the tree's origin book as its complete membership would produce large false-positive "extra talent" counts.

The highest-loss expansion lanes in the current repository remain:

- **Unknown Regions:** 25 of 30 checked additions are absent from the correct tree.
- **Galaxy of Intrigue:** 18 of 23 are absent from the correct tree.
- **Scavenger's Guide to Droids:** 16 of 23 are absent from the correct tree.
- **Galaxy at War:** 11 of 13 are absent from the correct tree.

By contrast:

- Rebellion Era: 8/8 structurally represented.
- Starships of the Galaxy: 10/10 structurally represented.
- Threats of the Galaxy: 1/1 represented.
- Jedi Academy: 22/22 represented.
- Scum and Villainy: all 55 mechanics represented in their intended expansion trees, subject to naming/identity cleanup already noted elsewhere.

KOTOR's 33 expansion claims include two source-confirmed Jedi Sentinel identities (`Sentinel Strike`, `Sentinel's Gambit`) currently claimed by Jedi Shadow, plus a `Sith Alchemy` naming/identity reconciliation case.

# Registry completion rule

After applying these publication edges, every primary-source verified tree should have:

- `originSourcebook`
- `originTalentNames`
- `talentPublications[]`
- `aggregateCanonicalTalentNames`
- source-scoped `accessPublications[]` where class access applies
- `aggregateClassAccess`
- special access rules for Force/tradition/droid-degree trees

`aggregateCanonicalTalentNames` is recomputed from publication provenance rather than maintained independently.
