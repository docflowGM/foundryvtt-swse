# Clone Wars — Vehicle-format mechanical source pass (M5, records 034–040)

Primary PDF printed pages 198–202 and 208 visually inspected. **Seven vehicle/starship records, zero promoted to individual droids.**

## CLONE-DROID-034 — Ground Armored Tank (p198)

Type ground-vehicle, CL 10, vehicle HP 175, DR 10, damage threshold 33, movement 8 squares (max velocity 130 km/h). Printed payload: missiles. Medium laser cannons (Atk +6, Dmg 4d10x2); Missile launchers (Atk +6, Dmg 8d6, 4-square splash).

- Vehicle brain (expert) controls this GAT; passenger count none. Do not convert it into a droid-actor with species feats or handheld weapons.
- Main ranged attack lines and pilot weapon table may differ in displayed bonuses; preserve separate fields and require canonical vehicle-weapon adjudication.
- Payload is 24 missiles, not inexhaustible attacks.

## CLONE-DROID-035 — IG-227 Hailfire-class Droid Tank (p199)

Type ground-vehicle, CL 12, vehicle HP 60, DR 10, damage threshold 29, movement 8 squares (max velocity 45 km/h). Printed payload: hailfire missiles. Missile launchers (Atk +5, Dmg 6d10x2, 4-square splash); Laser cannon (Atk +5, Dmg 4d10x2).

- Two enormous wheeled locomotion units and vehicle brain are vehicle mechanics, not personal droid appendages.
- 30 Hailfire missiles printed payload. Respect finite missile inventory and weapon fire-link rather than arbitrary repeating fire.
- Passenger and cargo none; Crew droid brain (skilled).

## CLONE-DROID-036 — Manta Droid Subfighter (p200)

Type water-vehicle, CL 9, vehicle HP 75, DR 10, damage threshold 27, movement swim 12 squares (max velocity 160 km/h). Printed payload: torpedoes. Laser cannons (Atk +3, Dmg 4d10x2); Torpedo launchers (Atk +3, Dmg 8d6, 4-square burst).

- Aquatic subfighter uses swim speed 12 and water-vehicle rules, not an ordinary hovering or walking actor.
- Torpedo payload exactly 12; fire-linked launchers do not create unlimited torpedoes.
- Droid brain normal and no passengers; all attacks under vehicle pilot/weapon ownership.

## CLONE-DROID-037 — OG-9 Homing Spider Droid (p201)

Type ground-vehicle, CL 7, vehicle HP 70, DR 10, damage threshold 27, movement 8 squares (max velocity 50 km/h). Printed payload: none stated. Laser cannon (Atk +6, Dmg 5d10); Heavy repeating blaster (Atk -1, autofire only, Dmg 3d10).

- A critical hit that equals/exceeds damage threshold can destroy legs and reduce speed to ZERO until repaired, while droid can continue firing; do not treat as automatic droid death.
- Dwarf spider droid spotters grant targeting assistance as separate cooperators; no unrestricted always-on +9 attack bonus.
- Heavy repeating blaster is autofire-only. Droid crew and starship weapons are vehicle authority.

## CLONE-DROID-038 — Persuader-class Droid Enforcer (p201)

Type ground-vehicle, CL 14, vehicle HP 120, DR 15, damage threshold 34, movement 6 squares (max velocity 50 km/h). Printed payload: thermal detonators (plus 12 concussion missiles, 4 homing missiles, 2 dumbfire torpedoes). Ion cannon (Atk +5, Dmg 4d10x2 ion); Heavy repeating blaster (Atk +0, autofire only, Dmg 3d10); Missile launchers (Printed attack table distinguishes thermal, concussion, homing and dumbfire munitions, separate payload and damage.).

- Payload categories are FINITE and distinct: 48 thermal detonators, 12 concussion missiles, four homing missiles, two dumbfire torpedoes.
- Vehicle Ion Cannon has ion type; heavy repeating blaster is autofire-only; missile launcher requires ammunition-type selection.
- Ground tracked enforcer is a vehicle crewed by skilled droid brain, not a droid actor with free held guns.

## CLONE-DROID-039 — Tri-Droid (p202)

Type ground-vehicle, CL 9, vehicle HP 160, DR 15, damage threshold 32, movement 8 squares (max velocity 50 km/h), climb 4 squares. Printed payload: heavy ordnance shells. Heavy ordnance launcher (Atk +4, Dmg 5d10x5, 4-square burst).

- Printed gargantuan walker uses ground-vehicle scale and climb 4; the visible three arms do not create three independent free actor attacks.
- Payload 48 heavy ordnance shells is finite; distinct crew/gunner action ownership from NPC droid feats.
- Tri-Droid is a separately published vehicle and must not be conflated with the Octuptarra Combat Tri-Droid droid actor on printed p196.

## CLONE-DROID-040 — Hyena-class Bomber (p208)

Type starfighter, CL 7, vehicle HP 70, DR 5, damage threshold 32, movement fly 16 squares (max velocity 1150 km/h), fly 4 squares (starship scale). Printed payload: proton torpedoes, plus 6 concussion missiles. Light laser cannons (Atk +7, Dmg 3d10x2); Proton torpedoes (Atk +7, Dmg 9d10x2); Light concussion missiles (Atk +7, Dmg 7d10x2).

- Two independent movement scales: fly 16 squares tactical and fly 4 squares starship; cannot apply 16 as starship flight.
- Droid bomber may use all its weapons in the same attack sequence as its own gunner under published source prose, subject to vehicle action authority; not ordinary individual hero multiattack.
- Payload 6 proton torpedoes and 6 concussion missiles, finite. Cargo none, passengers none, consumables two days.
