## Significant model boundaries

- **VX artillery droid:** missile launcher and separate light concussion weapon with finite possessed ammunition; source-only weapon operation.
- **ASN-121:** electronic Security Scan effect does not make a droid invisible to living guards; stabilized mount and carried sniper blaster require linking.
- **R3/R4/R5/R7:** store differently; R5's one hyperspace coordinate and separately modified owners are not default extra buffers, R7's non-E-wing skill penalty applies only while installed in another starfighter.
- **B3 vs B3-A:** remote/backup processor differs from heuristic/secondary battery; weapon burst modes and cartridges are conditional.
- **IG Lancer, Pistoeka, EVS:** lance reach and attack alternatives, discord-missile deployment reaction, wrecking-ball reach-only and object-damage footnotes remain model-specific.
- **GRZ-6B:** unresolved **`THREATS-013-PLASMA-WEAPON-NAME`**: attack plasma cannon versus possessed plasma torch as heavy blaster rifle. No invented arbitration.
- **TO-D, GH-7, EG-6, DRK-1:** stun-only sonic/medical combat restrictions, EG-6 generator Possession rather than infinite power, six possessed frag grenades, and print-limited stealth.
- **Generic Replica vs Guri:** generic model, Guri named individual, and separately printed Replica Droid Species Traits sidebar are distinct authorities, never duplicate actor identities or automatically combined progression.
- **Mark IV and SD-6:** Sentry has no factory firearm despite pistol proficiency; SD-6 has DR3, separate E-Webs and ion rifles, Controlled Burst/Delay Damage require canonical resolution.
- **Sith Elite Warbot:** Speed 6 printed but NO locomotion part in Systems, so source mode remains unspecified. Sith sword spending Force Point and increasing Dark Side Score is an optional conditioned weapon rule, not blanket passive damage.
- **PK Worker:** one unassigned feat and singular 'Mechanic' printed wording remain unarbitrated.
