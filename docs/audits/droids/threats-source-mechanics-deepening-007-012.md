# Threats of the Galaxy — Detailed source mechanics 007–012

**Source:** printed pp.139–143 (PDF pp.140–144); rendered primary images directly inspected. **Date:** 2026-11-02. No production or executable rules changes.

Six additional source identities now have 64 installed System instances, 26 physical appendage references, 17 separately reported Possessions, 29 grouped printed feats, 6 talents, 20 skill results, 25 source-only constraints and 19 non-executable attack alternatives.

## 007 — R2-R Reconnaissance Astromech, p139

This is a **scoundrel 1** printed model with Trace, not the stock R2 build. Ten-memory-unit astrogation buffer and separate data storage for **50 hours of holorecordings or 50,000 holo images** are Possessions. Two-kilogram internal storage is a different chassis System. The possessed hold-out blaster is not an integrated weapon. Basic is not a printed spoken language; two extra languages are *understand only*. Hero permission is not explicitly stated on this printed page.

## 008 — B3 Ultra Battle Droid, p140

Factory remote receiver **and** backup processor, six physical manipulators (two hands, four tools), quadanium +7 armor, hardened systems (2). Two heavy repeating blasters, flamethrower with **two** cartridges, and missile launcher with **two magazines** remain distinct possessions and finite source payload claims. Normal and Burst Fire attacks are alternatives. Hero prohibited.

## 009 — B3-A Ultra Battle Droid, p141

Print shows **soldier 5** with heuristic processor and a secondary battery, in contrast to B3's remote receiver/backup processor configuration. Three soldier talents and two heavy repeater attack alternatives are source claims. B3-A must not inherit B3's processor arrangement by accident. Hero prohibited.

## 010 — IG Series Battle Droid, p141

Energy lance is possessed and treated as a force pike with **+1 square reach**. Ordinary strike, Rapid Strike and combined Powerful Charge/Rapid Strike printed attacks are mutually alternative options, never free additional actions. No owned swoop or speeder is listed. No explicit hero permission on this page.

## 011 — Pistoeka Buzz Droid, p142

Its **flight 8/walk 4** systems and mounted tool arms are separate from the discord launcher sidebar. The launcher can release **three droids** per discord missile. The printed boarding reaction with **+5 equipment bonus** requires target-square contact and the proper vehicle encounter trigger; individual Pistoeka models do not automatically own a discord launcher or have a general free attack reaction. Vibrosaw and plasma torch are possessed items. Hero prohibited.

## 012 — EVS Construction Droid, p143

Colossal construction droid with **two claws and two tool mounts** in Systems and a separately possessed implosion wrecking ball, plasma cutter and duranium plating. The ball's printed ranged attack is **reach-limited** and deals **double damage only against objects, vehicles or structures**. Narrative describes a possible DC30 Use Computer/three-day hijack, not automatic factory permission for indiscriminate demolition. Hero prohibited.
