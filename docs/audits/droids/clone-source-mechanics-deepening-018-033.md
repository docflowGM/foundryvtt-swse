# Clone Wars droid source mechanical deepening M4 (018–033)

**Book:** Clone Wars Campaign Guide. **Primary printed pages:** 188–198. All eleven rendered source pages were reviewed, including separately laid-out simultaneous entries on 190, 192, 193, 194, 196 and 198. All 16 models remain droid-actor source records and **uncertified**.

## CLONE-DROID-018 — A-DSD Advanced Dwarf Spider Droid (p188)

Printed class line: nonheroic 9/soldier 3. Source Systems (6 independently named tokens): walking locomotion;basic processor;claw;improved sensor package;darkvision sensors;self-destruct system (8d6 damage). Printed possessions: blaster cannon;fire-linked missile launchers;36 frag missiles;quadanium battle armor.

- Leg Shields: MOVE action pulls legs inward, armor increases from +7 to +11 until start of next turn
- Stability +5 against being knocked prone
- Self-destruct 8d6 four-square burst consumes droid; fire-linked missile supply 36 frag missiles and is not infinite
- Cannot be heroic droid

## CLONE-DROID-019 — A-Series Assassin Droid (p189)

Printed class line: nonheroic 6/soldier 6/elite trooper 3. Source Systems (6 independently named tokens): walking locomotion;basic processor;claw;improved sensor package;darkvision sensors;self-destruct system (8d6 damage). Printed possessions: wrist blasters (as blaster rifle);finger blades;quadanium battle armor.

- Printed walking 6 and wheeled 8 are separate modes; repository system line appears to have previous-model OCR bleed and requires independent readback
- Finger blades and wrist blasters have alternate Rapid Strike, Rapid Shot and Double Attack modes, not free extra attacks
- Printed DR 1, not equivalent to generic shield SR
- Cannot be heroic droid

## CLONE-DROID-020 — B1-A Series Air Battle Droid (p190)

Printed class line: nonheroic 3/soldier 3. Source Systems (5 independently named tokens): walking locomotion;remote receiver;2 hand appendages;internal comlink;vocabulator. Printed possessions: 2 knives;2 wrist blasters (as blaster pistols);jet pack (10 charges).

- Flight 6 squares comes from finite jet pack, distinct from walking 6; no unbounded flying
- Two knives and two wrist blasters are distinct held/equipped arms with alternative multiple-attack options
- Can use jet pack 10 charges as written, do not grant 10 independent consumable powers
- Cannot be heroic droid

## CLONE-DROID-021 — Battle Droid Assassin (p190)

Printed class line: nonheroic 3/soldier 3. Source Systems (7 independently named tokens): walking locomotion;remote receiver;synchronized fire circuits;2 hand appendages;improved sensor package;internal comlink;vocabulator. Printed possessions: heavy blaster rifle with standard targeting scope;hold-out blaster pistol;2 frag grenades.

- Heavy blaster rifle has separate Devastating Attack and Deadeye interactions; not an unconditional always-on damage bonus
- Frag grenades are finite two units and 1-square ranged burst only under applicable rules
- Weapon focus/scope is held inventory, not a built-in rifle mount
- Cannot be heroic droid

## CLONE-DROID-022 — Cold Assault Battle Droid (p191)

Printed class line: nonheroic 6. Source Systems (8 independently named tokens): walking locomotion;remote receiver;backup processor;synchronized fire circuits;2 hand appendages;internal comlink;vocabulator;plasteel shell (+2 armor). Printed possessions: heavy blaster rifle.

- Can move up to three times normal speed when running on snow/ice, NOT four; model/environment-specific
- Backup processor and remote receiver both listed; do not treat as two fully independent minds or grant free remote authority
- Uses dangerous heavy blaster rifle and Rapid Shot alternative; no unlimited external firing
- Cannot be heroic droid

## CLONE-DROID-023 — C-B3 Cortosis Battle Droid (p192)

## CLONE-DROID-024 — Droid Commando (p192)

- Printed Force 3 and Combat Analysis talent with battle analysis/cover fire not a generic party bonus
- Precision Rifle/Sniper feat conditions must be satisfied; no automatic unseen-target accuracy

- Cannot be heroic droid

## CLONE-DROID-025 — IG Lancer Combat Droid (p193)

Printed class line: nonheroic 6. Source Systems (5 independently named tokens): walking locomotion;remote receiver;2 hand appendages;internal comlink;vocabulator. Printed possessions: energy lance (as force pike, +1 square reach).

- Printed energy lance provides +1 reach, not baseline lengthening of all melee weapons
- Powerful Charge and Power Attack options have distinct modifiers; foot droid does not automatically own its illustration's swoop bike
- This listed IG lancer is hero-prohibited

## CLONE-DROID-026 — IG-110 Lightsaber Droid (p193)

Printed class line: soldier 1/Jedi 5. Source Systems (8 independently named tokens): walking locomotion;magnetic feet;heuristic processor;2 hand appendages;improved sensor package;locked access;secondary battery;vocabulator. Printed possessions: 2 lightsabers;quadanium armor.

- Dual Weapon Mastery and Rapid Strike are attack alternatives with two PHYSICAL lightsabers, not free iterative attacks
- Printed soldier/Jedi levels simulate Jedi training; source explicitly states droid is NOT a Jedi
- Both lightsabers are possessions and need legal grip/weapon resource checks
- Hero use prohibited

## CLONE-DROID-027 — JK-13 Security Droid (p194)

Printed class line: soldier 12. Source Systems (7 independently named tokens): walking locomotion (extra legs);climbing claws;organic processor;10 probes;hardened systems (x3);shield generator (SR20);improved sensor package. Printed possessions: stun tentacles.

- Killing Rage at 0 HP: no longer takes attack and damage penalties but CANNOT Use the Force, attacks nearest friend OR foe until destroyed
- Stability +5 to resist prone
- Compare attack to target Fortitude and Reflex; if it hits both defenses target moves one step down condition track
- Organic Force-sensitive dashta eel processor is not ordinary heuristic processor; printed Force 2 does not make heroic species/PC legal

## CLONE-DROID-028 — LM-432 Assault Crab Droid (p194)

## CLONE-DROID-029 — LR-57 Combat Droid (p195)

Printed class line: nonheroic 9. Source Systems (8 independently named tokens): walking locomotion;basic processor;remote receiver;2 tool mounts;improved sensor package;internal comlink;locked access;vocabulator. Printed possessions: double heavy blaster rifle;durasteel plating (+6 armor).

- Source describes buried ambush: sensor activation and firing upon detecting target, not a free out-of-turn attack independent of initiative
- Double heavy blaster rifle normal +7 vs Burst Fire +2 are attack alternatives
- Remote receiver and basic processor coexist as printed; unverified remote-order logic
- Hero use prohibited

## CLONE-DROID-030 — Octuptarra Combat Tri-Droid (p196)

Printed class line: nonheroic 9. Source Systems (6 independently named tokens): walking locomotion (extra legs);magnetic feet;basic processor;internal comlink;improved sensor package;self-destruct system (8d6 damage or virus). Printed possessions: 3 laser turrets.

- VIRUS alternative self destruct: attack against each living target Fortitude within burst; hit = two persistent steps down condition track; miss = one persistent step, reoccurs daily until treated
- Choose virus or explosive self-destruct as published; not both simultaneously or unlimited repeated use
- Three laser turrets have multiple printed Burst/Rapid/Double attack alternatives, not automatic extra owned actions
- Hero use prohibited

## CLONE-DROID-031 — OOM-Series Battle Droid (p196)

Printed class line: nonheroic 6. Source Systems (5 independently named tokens): walking locomotion;remote receiver;2 hand appendages;internal comlink;vocabulator. Printed possessions: blaster carbine;electrobinoculars (commander only);personal holoprojector (pilot only).

- Commander versions receive additional Coordinated Attack and Knowledge (tactics) training, not all stock OOM units
- Pilot model receives Skill Training (Pilot), not all OOM models
- Radio/remote receiver does not guarantee autonomous fallback when cut off from remote controller
- Hero use prohibited

## CLONE-DROID-032 — Spelunker Probe Droid (p197)

- Climb locomotion of 3 squares separate from walking 6
- Hero use prohibited

## CLONE-DROID-033 — Ultra Droideka (p198)

Printed class line: nonheroic 16. Source Systems (6 independently named tokens): walking locomotion;wheeled locomotion (exclusive);remote receiver;2 tool appendages;shield generator (SR20);integrated comlink. Printed possessions: 2 heavy repeating blasters (treat as E-web repeating blasters);durasteel plating (+6 armor).

- Wheeled 12 speed and walking 3 speed are exclusive travel modes; do not stack locomotion
- SR20 shield does not authorize firing while moving in wheeled form without published conditions
- Two heavy repeating blasters and Burst Fire/Double/dual-weapon modes remain source-only until mount and ammo ownership reconciled
- Hero use prohibited

## Integrity and limits

16 printed identity/name/page checks and source citations performed. The B1-A (p190) and Battle Droid Assassin (p190) are separate. All printed attack groups and extra mechanics remain **source-only**. The review records 16 class lines, installed-System named evidence, stock possession lines and conditional mechanics; it does NOT establish canonical feats/talent IDs, installed mount permission or combat execution. Full Python schema validation, Foundry runtime testing and production mutations NOT performed.
