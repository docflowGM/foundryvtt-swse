# Clone Wars droid source mechanics — Sprint 011–017 (M3)

Primary PDF pages visually inspected: printed 72–73, 94, 160–162. **Seven source identities (6 individuals, 1 squad)**, including FX-6 two-page split. Source-only evidence; no certification or production edits.

## CLONE-DROID-011 — CLL-6 Binary Load Lifter (printed 72)

- **NO-FINE-MANIPULATORS:** The printed two tool appendages are load-lifting trays; prose explicitly denies hand/manipulator use. Do not grant free precision manipulation or an additional independent limb.
- **BASIC-RECEPTION-ONLY:** Basic understand-only, speaks Binary; no Basic speech from order reception.
- **HERO-PROHIBITED:** CLL-6 is explicitly unavailable as a droid hero.

## CLONE-DROID-012 — FA-5 Valet Droid (printed 72)

- **PILOT-PROGRAMMING-NOT-VEHICLE:** Pilot protocols allow parking/retrieving owners' ships or airspeeders through actual vehicle controls; no vehicle attack, special mount or own vehicle actor granted.
- **UNASSIGNED-FEATS:** Two printed feats are expressly UNASSIGNED; not two freely selected certified combat feats. Legal class/feat assignment requires downstream proof.
- **HERO-ELIGIBLE:** Hero creation allowed, but printed nonheroic level and factory Pilot check not free heroic progression.

## CLONE-DROID-013 — IW-37 Pincer Loader Droid (printed 73)

- **LOADER-WEAPON-CONTEXT:** Stock two claws have printed melee 2 claws +10 (1d6+7); cargo/towing descriptions do not create torpedo/missile launch capability or a vehicle weapon mount.
- **ARMOR-POSSESSION-RELATION:** Factory Systems identify durasteel plating (+6 armor) but Possessions ALSO lists durasteel shell; preserve dual source claims for physical armor reconciliation, do not double count armor bonuses.
- **HERO-PROHIBITED:** IW-37 explicitly may not be a heroic droid.

## CLONE-DROID-014 — B1-Series Battle Droid Squad (printed 94)

- **SQUAD-FOUR-BASE:** Derived from four B1 droids; construction increases size one category and doubles source character hit points, increases damage threshold by 10, adds +4 on all attack rolls, and treats units as aiding each other. This is SQUAD packaging, not four distinct turn owners.
- **SQUAD-MELEE-AREA:** Squad melee attacks affect all squares within its reach (unless it elects not to affect all), not four individual attacks.
- **SQUAD-RANGED-SPLASH:** Squad ranged attacks have 1-square splash and splash damage equals 2 dice; eligible attacks still require actual issued squad weapons.
- **SQUAD-INTERACTION:** Squad cannot be grabbed or grappled, can make opportunity attacks that provoke it but those are not area attacks; squad has 2x2 fighting space.
- **FOUR-INDIVIDUALS-NOT-STOCK-CHASSIS:** This source identity represents a group of four units in one squad statblock. Do not grant individual droid hero creation or duplicate squad hp/attack statistics to a single B1.

## CLONE-DROID-015 — FX-6 Medical Assistance Droid (printed 160–161)

- **SIX-INSTRUMENTS-NOT-SIX-HANDS:** Six instruments in factory Systems are medical instruments, not proven six generic manipulators, hands, or mounted attacks. Main statblock 'Melee instrument +1 (1d2-1)' remains source-only.
- **SURGERY-PRIORITY:** Printed programming prefers cybernetic intervention if viable/cost-effective; this is descriptive priority, not a free surgery, replacement inventory or additional health restoration.
- **MEDICAL-KIT-POSSESSION:** Medical kit is carried under Possessions rather than an installed Systems component.
- **SPLIT-PAGE-FIELDS:** The FX-6 statblock begins on p160 and continues on p161; source evidence for every printed feat, movement, Systems, and Possessions is p161. Do not treat the continuation as an absent field.

## CLONE-DROID-016 — FA-4 Pilot Droid (printed 161)

- **PRIVACY-WIPE:** Narrative says FA-4 owner may wipe destination/conversation memory via voice command; there is no stated free shield/weapon immunity or unrestricted remote hacking permission.
- **CANNOT-FIRE-SHIP-WEAPONS:** Although trained to pilot and avoid hazards, the published FA-4 is NOT programmed for combat and cannot use its ship weaponry to fire back. Vehicular Combat is defensive, not a weapon right.
- **BUFFER-CAPACITY:** Ten-memory-unit astrogation buffer printed as a possession/storage device, not ten kilograms and not a built-in vehicle.
- **HERO-ELIGIBLE:** Heroic route allowed, no automatic nonheroic 2 or factory feat grants.

## CLONE-DROID-017 — Marksman-H Combat Remote (printed 162)

- **STUN-ONLY-FACTORY:** Ranged light blaster +5 deals 1d4 STUN; factory low-power stunner blaster must not automatically grant standard lethal damage output.
- **ACTIVATION-TRIGGER:** Narrative default setting remains on guard until target readies weapon, then begins shooting; no unlimited attack as a free reaction without ordinary encounter turn authority.
- **STABILIZED-MOUNT:** A real stabilized weapon mount is printed, and light blaster is Possession; must bind actual mount-to-weapon relationship before executing an attack.
- **HERO-PROHIBITED:** This Diminutive combat remote cannot be a heroic droid.

## Checkpoint boundary

The B1 aggregation represents four B1 units, doubles hit points, adds +10 damage threshold and +4 attack, and imposes distinct melee-area/ranged-splash rules; none of these translate into four independent actor turns. FA-4 cannot fire its ship's weapons, and Marksman-H's low-power blaster is stun-only. FX-6 instruments and medical kit remain separate source facts.
