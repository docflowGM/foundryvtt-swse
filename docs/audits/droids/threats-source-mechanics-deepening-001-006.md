# Threats of the Galaxy — Source-mechanical deepening 001–006

**Date:** 2026-11-02 | **Branch:** `audit/droid-publication-census-2026-10-29` | **Printed pages:** 134–138 (PDF 135–139).

The third book in the droid priority queue has started. Six distinct model statblocks were directly reviewed using rendered source pages. Added 79 factory System instances, 34 separately locatable appendages, 26 separate possession entries, 36 grouped printed feats, 11 grouped printed talents, 27 skills, 24 bounded source constraints and 8 non-executable attack rows. No individual receives strict mechanical certification.

## THREATS-DROID-001 — VX Series Artillery Droid (printed p134)

Two tool appendages plus tracked locomotion, processor, sensor and communications Systems. Separate Possessions list **two missile launchers/four magazines** and **light concussion missile launcher with two light concussion missiles**. The equipment sidebar defines the light launcher independently. Its two ranged statblock attacks do not establish hand/grip/mount or unlimited ammunition. Soldier 12 and six talents / twelve printed feat groups are not a playable chassis: explicitly hero-prohibited.

## THREATS-DROID-002 — ASN-121 Assassin Droid (printed p135)

Flying 9, one claw plus two tools, one stabilized weapon mount, 2kg internal storage and separate sniper blaster (treated as a heavy blaster pistol). *Immune to Security Scans* applies to electronic observation as described, **not invisibility to living observers**. The mount-to-weapon attachment remains uncertified; the assassin is hero-prohibited.

## THREATS-DROID-003 — R3 Series Astromech Droid (printed p136)

Wheeled 6 / walking 4. Six tool appendages, one claw and telescopic appendage are individually retained. Internal 2kg storage is distinct from the possessed five-memory-unit astrogation buffer, with five possible hyperspace coordinate sets in the narrative. Tools and electronics remain possessions rather than implied extra limbs or weapons.

## THREATS-DROID-004 — R4 Series Astromech Droid (printed p137)

Wheeled 6 / walking 4 with 8kg internal storage. No printed magnetic feet or improved sensor package. Toolkit is a possession. Starfighter-specific R4 variants described in prose are **not the factory stock configuration**.

## THREATS-DROID-005 — R5 Series Astromech Droid (printed p138)

Wheeled 6 / walking 4, six tools, claw and telescoping arm. Main narrative restricts factory hyperspace memory to **one coordinate set**, and no astrogation buffer is in Possessions. Later Rebels modified individual R5s to six, seven, or ten sets; no universal grant or invented installation procedure.

## THREATS-DROID-006 — R7 Series Astromech Droid (printed p138)

Wheeled 6 / walking 4 with **four**, not six, tool appendages and just 1kg internal storage. Narrative says it can store 15 hyperspace coordinate sets. The printed **−4 to all skill checks in fighters other than the E-wing** is recorded only in that conditional installed-ship context, never as a global penalty. Hero-eligible but printed nonheroic 6 stats are not free starting-class rights.
