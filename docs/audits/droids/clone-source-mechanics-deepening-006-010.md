## 006 P2 Series Astromech Droid
Two distinct speeds: wheeled 8, walking 2. Six tool and two claw appendages are individual installed components. Basic understand-only, no vocabulator; dome screen communications. Astrogation buffer with FOUR memory units is a Possession rather than installed 10kg storage. Carried saw, welder, extinguisher, holoprojector and holorecorder do not imply free attacks.

## 007 Pit Droid
Small droid with NO Small-size carrying capacity penalty; folds into Tiny OBJECT only when deactivated. Basic understand-only. Generic ranged by weapon +3 has no printed stock firearm or mount. Walking speed 4.

## 008 5YQ Protocol Droid
Factory translator DC10, 5kg internal storage, carried audio recorder. Optional published removal of creative dampeners permits falsehoods and independent ideas but has NO printed mechanical DC, cost, time or Deception bonus. Source-only variant; no free programming grant.

## 009 BCA-11/X Training Droid

## 010 IG-86 Sentinel Droid

Attack names BLASTER CARBINE but Possessions names BLASTER RIFLE, unresolved CLONE-010-BLASTER-CARBINE-RIFLE-CONFLICT. Durasteel shell is a Possession, not a listed installed System; no automatic armor or weapon permission. Hero-eligible does not inherit its printed feat/talent grants.
