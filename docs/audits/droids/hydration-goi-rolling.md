## Source review boundaries

- **GOI-DROID-001**: External warning bracelet or earbud is not necessarily an installed chassis system.
- **GOI-DROID-002**: Narrative calls model unarmed; printed statblock has a ranged blaster attack and a blaster possession: unresolved source discrepancy.
- **GOI-DROID-002**: Self-destruct is a conditional printed action, not unconditional attack availability.
- **GOI-DROID-003**: Narrative says stock chassis unarmed, but shown example possesses a concealed hold-out blaster. Factory baseline cannot inherit it automatically.
- **GOI-DROID-004**: Blaster licensing changes if weapon retained; do not assume weapon remains installed for every sale.
- **GOI-DROID-005**: Footman glyph varies across OCR (FIII/Fill); keep original source identity and do not create Fidelis/Solis separate statblocks.
- **GOI-DROID-006**: Rail cannon/blaster cannon treatment is model-specific; no mount inferred.
- **GOI-DROID-007**: Two pistol possessions do not grant a second executable attack.
- **GOI-DROID-008**: Self-destruct splash and tactical HP trigger must be kept source-scoped; stability legs and claw appendages are separate counts.

## Remaining certification gates

Full field-by-field verification, secondary presentations, exact feat/talent identity, equipment provenance, attack mounts, PC legality, upstream schema validation and Foundry runtime testing. **None of these are certified by this pass.**
