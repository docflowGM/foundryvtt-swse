## 036 SD-X-Series Stealth Battle Droid (pp.138–139)

Five actions: move, possessed blaster rifle, conditional Stealth, concealed built-in finger vibrodaggers and Threat Scan. **New `SGD-036-THREAT-SCAN-ACTION-CONFLICT`:** main scan is full-round while protocol is two swift; DC20 Perception and 30m general location/direction details. Blade extension separately swift, multi-dagger damage already included (4d4+7). Stealth coating +5 in darkness or shadow only; sensor opposition vs Use Computer.

**Bodyguard Enhancements:** DC25 Use Computer/2,000-credit program links scan and a reroll on one attack against scanned threat once per encounter; optional Harm's Way+Trip reaction requires real feat and target adjacency and knocks both ally and defender prone. **Martial Arts Enhancements:** REPLACE Weapon Proficiency pistols and Precise Shot with Martial Arts III and Dual Weapon Mastery I, not additive. Optional DC25 Use Computer/3,000-credit program permits unarmed-damage addition to a successful vibrodagger hit Dex modifier times/encounter (min one).

Hero route: stock battle chassis with laminanium/heuristic upgrade and soldier base, later scout/Skill Training (Stealth); does not start with all printed levels and trained Stealth.

## 037 LEP-Series Service Droid (pp.140–141)

Six actions: move, Empathic (Persuasion attitude reroll, keep better), First Aid (adjacent DC15, once/day/patient), Haggle (+2 to master's Persuasion after successful check), Revivify (died within prior round; actual medical authority required), Treat Poison.

**Bodyguard:** hardened systems x2, electric defense grid; REPLACE Skill Focus Persuasion/Treat Injury with proficiencies for separately provided melee/pistol gear. **Nanny:** swap Skill Focus Perception to Improved Defenses; childproof lock, optional Knowledge (social sciences), optional breathable hidden-child compartment requiring two DC15 Mechanics checks, 1.5h. **Personal Transport:** secondary repulsorlift, fold-down reinforced hatch up to 200kg plus passenger control stick; not stock 30kg internal storage expansion or a created vehicle.

Hero route: stock service chassis swaps tool mount to two probes and Empathic, noble/Linguist suggestions are legal class/feat options, not granted inventory.

## 038 Shadow Droid (pp.142–143)

Five vehicle-oriented actions: move fly16 tactical/fly4 starship, Engage Enemy Pilot opposed check, Protect and Serve (spends **owner's** Force Point to help adjacent character), Ramming Speed Pilot -10 vs target starship Reflex, damage 4d6+10 to BOTH craft on successful contact, half to both on failure; Wingman +2 to pilot check.

**Fear Generator:** Sith Use the Force vs droid Will/2h, DC25 Persuasion/30m; droid standard-action Persuasion vs adjacent target Will once per target per encounter induces mind-affecting -1 condition. **Sith Squadron:** Sith Force check vs droid Will/1h and DC20 Mechanics uplink/30m; aid another to attuned master +4 instead of +2.

Shadow Droid is **hero-prohibited** and flight/ship weapon payload belongs to vehicle authority, not an ordinary extra ranged-attack grant. Proton torpedoes 4 and concussion missiles 8 are finite.

## 039 Colicoid T4 Turret Droid (pp.144–145)

Five protocol actions: move, Cutting Laser five contiguous squares (area, +9 vs Reflex, DR ignored, half on miss), Patrol Duty, Rapid Shot, Stability. **Existing `SGD-039-CUTTING-LASER-ACTION-CONFLICT` retained**: main standard vs two swift protocol, unresolved.

**Artillery Spotter:** improved sensors/comlink/synchronized fire plus retraining Weapon Focus and Skill Focus toward Perception. **Listening Post:** sensors/booster, long-range comlink, DC5 translator, hypertransceiver DC30 Mechanics/2 days; retrain Rapid Shot to Skill Training Perception and Skill Focus Initiative to Perception. **Patrol Guard:** walking speed to 8 DC25 Mechanics/one day, low-power spotlight cutting laser DC15 Mechanics/1h; on spotlight hit blind for margin above Reflex number of rounds. These variants cannot be treated as factory upgrades. Two separate blaster cannons and grenade launcher carrying **four** thermal detonators are finite mounts/possession evidence; hero use prohibited.

## 040 TC-SC Infiltration Droid (pp.146–147)

Five actions: move, Avoid Surprise, Dastardly Blade, Dastardly Blast, Patrol Duty. Knife/blaster are separate concealed possessions; Dastardly Strike condition movement only on successful attack **and** target denied Dexterity bonus to Reflex.

**Decoy:** DC25 Use Computer/1h template, DC25 Use Computer after 1+ days observing target (+1/day up to INT bonus), DC20 Mechanics/30m to attach disguiser emergency power. Disguise persists until single damage exceeds Will Defense or unit reaches 0 HP. **Elimination:** DC25 Use Computer/1h image, DC25 Use Computer/30m mimic target skills by **replacing** one feat, then DC20 Use Computer/1h schematic; only specific target droid's damage threshold treated 5 lower against this infiltrator's attacks. Separate lawful scoundrel/scout player routes; no free factory skill-focus feats.
