## 6. Representative builds against the real tag-signal path

Running the same builds in a live Foundry world is required for that comparison.

What can be stated from code instead: `tag-signal-engine.buildTagBiasMap` max-merges its sources at fixed scales (identity 1.0x, mentor biases 1.1x, archetype 1.15x, themes 1.0, route confidence up to 0.95). The survey channel is delivered through the same max-merge at a hard ceiling of 0.5, which is below each of those scales, so it can add a ranking signal but cannot out-weigh a primary observed route or archetype signal on its own.

## 7. Findings and limits

1. The hierarchy requested by the owner holds: Prestige > Multiclass > L1 initially; L1 > Multiclass > Prestige in persistence. The original illustrative A values (1.00 / 0.95 / 0.90) are not used.
2. Ordering-before/after: with no certified answer-to-tag mapping the survey channel is empty for every golden build, so Suggestion Engine inputs are byte-identical with and without surveys (`tests/mentor-survey-influence-integration.test.mjs`). The legacy path (shared decay with a 0.175 floor, additive per-answer maps) is removed and no longer reaches the engine.
3. **Not validated:** real Suggestion Engine ranking behavior with certified survey tags cannot be measured until the answer-to-tag mapping is certified, and the offline harness cannot produce observed-behavior weights (section 6). The only supported claim is structural: the survey channel is hard-bounded below the fixed scales of the other tag sources.
4. **Cap sensitivity (owner attention):** with these defaults the per-tag raw cap (2.0) is reached by just two strong events agreeing on the same tag (section 5), after which further events add nothing. That is conservative against volume, but it may also flatten genuine long-term reinforcement. Raise `channel.maxRawPerTag` if that is not wanted.
5. The channel cap (2.0 raw, 0.5 suggestion ceiling) and `k = 1` are placeholders chosen to keep the survey channel below observed-behavior weights; they are not calibrated. They need owner review once real mappings exist.
6. Q6 correction coefficients are deliberately unset (`calibration.negativeFactorByCalibration = null`): corrections are stored and reported, never applied, until the owner approves values.
