## Decision and selection contract

- **Q3 is a class-development situation, selected from the player's exact Q1 answer.** It does **not** select from Q2's career parent and does not assume any career was selected.
- **Q4 is the parent-career situation**, selected independently from the explicit Q2 parent ID; Q4's selection must not depend on the Q3 answer.
- Five substantive Q1 choices per foundation class receive one unique, concrete Q3 situational question. Each class also has an **undecided fallback**, because every Q1 choice must have a Q3 route. **30 scenarios total: 25 directed + 5 neutral.**
- The selected class Mentor's stage introduction is displayed separately from the situational prompt. The existing Q3 Mentor stage introductions that imply a chosen profession need targeted revision to class-situation wording; this bank does not quietly alter their approved text.
- Each Q1 answer has **one distinct narrative situation**, followed by the exact same player-facing prompt: **“What would you do?”**. Custom situation-by-situation interrogatives are superseded. The first-person response choices are not yet authored. Five discriminating method choices should be developed and canonically validated after scenario review. No scenario presumes a feat, talent, trained skill, equipment item, Force power, or rank has already been acquired.
- Direction titles and Q1-answer mappings are **developer-only metadata**; no Q1 role labels, metadata, scores, IDs, or taxonomy appear in the player response list.

## Editorial QA / next decisions

1. **Coverage:** Exactly six Q1 routes for each of five classes, 30 scenario identities total; the 25 directed scenarios correspond to one substantive Q1 development answer each.
2. **No implicit parent selection:** Not one scenario is derived from a Q2 parent career; Q3 can still operate if the player opted out of Q2.
3. **Player skill and preference:** Prompts avoid claims of currently granted talents, feats, or Force powers. A scenario can supply temporary environmental equipment or a fictional assigned duty; such fiction must not be mistaken for permanent inventory or an acquired proficiency.
4. **Response authoring pending:** For each situation, write five first-person method choices that produce independently meaningful, class-compatible preference evidence. Do **not** make all responses confirm the Q1 direction; Q3 should test whether the player would choose a different method under pressure.
5. **Q3 mentor voice pending:** Revise Q3 stage lines as a separate approval task to accurately introduce class rather than career situations. Q4 remains the career-context stage.
6. **No numeric scoring:** Exact Q3 intent tags, mechanical reference mapping, and weighting must be source-validated before implementation.
7. **No implementation:** These are narrative design prompts, not loaded survey data or production-ready code.
