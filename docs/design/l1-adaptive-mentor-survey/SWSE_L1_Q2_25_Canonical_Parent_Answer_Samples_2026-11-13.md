# SWSE Level 1 Adaptive Mentor Survey — Q2 Career Calling

**Local design draft · November 13, 2026 · Not committed or pushed**

## Scope

Twenty-five first-person candidate Q2 career-aspiration answers. Each maps one-to-one to a **real canonical parent** in `data/archetypes.json`, verified read-only from `docflowGM/foundryvtt-swse` default branch (file blob SHA `76b1ee3aa06dcb29a88ff4ca69bbc8b96cebc3fe`). These are a diverse **example pool for the Noble Q2 discussion**, not a claim that all twenty-five would be selected by one ranker, nor that they must appear all at once. Q2 presents approximately five suggested answers plus a non-voting browse/navigation choice; subsequent browsed answers are still Q2, not Q3.

All parent names and identifiers below are **designer-only metadata**, not player-visible labels. A player's selected parent records declared career intent, not ownership of its skills, talents, feats, prestige classes, or other mechanics. Class/species/attributes/Q1 affect ordering, never eligibility. Merely displaying or browsing an answer records no preference.

## Design status

- **Proposed wording**, not yet approved or production certified.
- Intended to inform authoring of response text for canonical parents, not to create a parallel archetype registry.
- Career descriptions should be reviewed for player tone and meaningful distinction; canonical scoring and parent ranking remain open in the approved L1 rolling reference v0.10.
- Do not encode these twenty-five examples as fixed Noble answers. Q2 is shared in purpose across five starting classes and draws from the same parent authority.
- The actual character sees only quoted response sentences; no headings, archetype names, slot titles, tag strings, weights, or mechanical scores.
