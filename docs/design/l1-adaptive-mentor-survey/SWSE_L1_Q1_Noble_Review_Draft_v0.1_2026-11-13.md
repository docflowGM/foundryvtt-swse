## Canonical Noble class baseline

Primary source: *Star Wars Saga Edition Core Rulebook*, Noble class and its talent trees. Text checked against `/mnt/data/Core Rulebook_djvu.txt` on 2026-11-13.

- Starting hit points: 18 + Constitution modifier; subsequent Noble levels 1d6 + Constitution modifier.
- Class Defense bonuses: +1 Reflex, +2 Will.
- Starting feats: Weapon Proficiency (pistols); Weapon Proficiency (simple weapons); Linguist only when Intelligence 13 prerequisite is met. Automatic grants are capabilities, not additional preference votes.
- Trained class skills: 6 + Intelligence modifier. Class list: Deception, Gather Information, Initiative, Knowledge (individual specialties), Perception, Persuasion, Pilot, Ride, Treat Injury, Use Computer.
- Core Noble talent trees: Influence (persuasion, intimidation, coercive social effects); Inspiration (bolstering and encouraging allies); Leadership (combat coordination, authority, team support); Lineage (connections, education, skill flexibility, and wealth).
- A Noble selects a talent at Noble 1 and subsequent odd Noble class levels. Even Noble levels supply a Noble bonus feat from the printed class list, subject to prerequisites.
- This is a skill-rich support and social class, not merely nobility by birth; Core describes diplomats, merchants, commanders, corporate magnates, political figures, crime lords, and other roles.

## Proposed Q1 prompt

**Which aspect of your Noble training would you most like to develop first?**

| Visible choice label | First-person candidate answer | Support and distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Officer / Leader | I want to learn how to lead others, make decisions under pressure, and bring a team through difficult situations. | Leadership tree; Coordinate/Born Leader. More command-centered than advisory planning. |
| Diplomat | I want to become a skilled negotiator, influencing outcomes through conversation, judgment, and understanding. | Persuasion/Gather Information; Influence tree. Not restricted to peaceful altruism; Influence also includes intimidation. |
| Tactician | I want to anticipate problems, coordinate the right response, and give my allies an advantage through preparation. | Knowledge, Initiative, Leadership's coordination features; planning rather than command identity. |
| Mastermind | I want to understand the people and systems around me so I can shape events without relying on force. | Deception, Gather Information, Influence, and Lineage's Connections. A thematic development direction, not a Core talent tree name. |
| Public Figure / Patron (existing label: Celebrity) | I want to build a network of connections and resources, and learn to use my position to open doors for myself and others. | Lineage (Connections, Wealth, Educated); more accurate mechanical differentiation than a narrowly entertainment-only celebrity label. |
| I'm not sure yet | I'm not sure yet. I'd like to discover what my abilities can offer before deciding where to concentrate my efforts. | Explicit neutral response; no Noble-specific positive/negative preference weight. |
