## Jedi — Miraj

**Mentor:** "You needn't know every step of your journey today. Even the most certain among us can find ourselves somewhere unexpected. But tell me, who do you hope to become?"

1. "I want to understand the Force deeply enough to accomplish things I can barely imagine today, and use that understanding to help others."
2. "I want to master my lightsaber and become someone who can stand against overwhelming danger without allowing others to suffer."
3. "I want to recognize threats that others overlook and uncover mysteries that could change the lives of countless people."
4. "I want to develop the wisdom and influence to resolve conflicts without always needing to draw a weapon."
5. "I want to become a well-rounded Jedi, capable of adapting to whatever challenges the galaxy places before me."

**Editorial intent directions:** Force mastery/support; lightsaber mastery/defense; investigation/perception; persuasion/leadership; versatility/adaptability.
