# SWSE Level 1 Adaptive Mentor Survey - Working Mentor Dialogue Draft v0.3

**Date:** 2026-11-13  
**Status:** Local-only design reference. J0-N1 Q1-Q5 expressly approved by owner; Miraj, Ol' Salty, Lead, and Breach lines recorded as current working selections, subject to final sign-off.  
**GitHub:** No commit, push, or remote change authorized.  
**Scope:** 25 fixed stage-framing lines, five mentors times five L1 questions. These are Mentor lines, not the adaptive substantive question/answer text.

## Structure

- Q1 - Class Development: Which direction within the chosen starting class to cultivate first.
- Q2 - Career Calling: Which long-term professional calling to pursue; full canonical parent-archetype registry remains available.
- Q3 - First Career Situation: Introduces one hypothetical situation related to Q2.
- Q4 - Second Career Situation: Introduces a separate, nonredundant situation, selected without using the Q3 answer.
- Q5 - Long-Term Ambition: What the character hopes to become.

## 1. Miraj - Jedi

**Q1 - Class Development**

> "Before we begin, tell me what you'd like to learn. Not what you believe you're supposed to be good at, but what you genuinely wish to understand."

**Q2 - Career Calling**

> "Now, answer honestly. I'm not looking for the answer you think I want to hear. What sort of life would you choose for yourself?"

**Q3 - First Career Situation**

> "Let us imagine you've begun following that path. Something happens that requires a decision, and the choice is yours to make. How would you respond?"

**Q4 - Second Career Situation**

> "Very well. Now consider something different. What served you before may not serve you here, so tell me what you would do."

**Q5 - Long-Term Ambition**

> "You needn't know every step of your journey today. Even the most certain among us can find ourselves somewhere unexpected. But tell me, who do you hope to become?"

## 2. J0-N1 - Noble (OWNER APPROVED)

**Q1 - Class Development**

> "Inquiry: Master, which of your abilities would you most like to cultivate? I can assess your aptitudes with considerable accuracy. Your personal interests, however, require your input."

**Q2 - Career Calling**

> "Observation: The most efficient career is not necessarily the most fulfilling one, Master. I would be interested to know which profession you would choose for yourself."

**Q3 - First Career Situation**

> "Hypothetical Scenario: Let us assume you have successfully entered your chosen profession, Master. An unexpected complication has arisen. How would you prefer to resolve it?"

**Q4 - Second Career Situation**

> "Cautionary Observation: A different set of circumstances now requires your attention, Master. I advise against assuming that a familiar approach is always the correct one. How would you proceed?"

**Q5 - Long-Term Ambition**

> "Personal Admission: I can calculate the probability of your success, Master. I find myself rather more concerned with whether that success would make you happy. Who do you hope to become?"

## 3. Ol' Salty - Scoundrel

**Q1 - Class Development**

> "Arr! Pick yer path wisely, matey! The right tricks make the difference between a chest o' credits and a long walk out a short airlock."

**Q2 - Career Calling**

> "Every spacer's chasin' somethin', matey. Credits, glory, freedom, maybe a ship worth callin' yer own. So tell ol' Salty, what sort o' life are ye chartin' a course for?"

**Q3 - First Career Situation**

> "A fancy ship won't save ye if ye can't lie, sneak, or think fast! Now, every good job's got a way o' turnin' sour. Let's see how ye'd handle a little trouble."

**Q4 - Second Career Situation**

> "Yar! But there ain't just one kind o' trouble in this galaxy, matey. Let's throw another scrape yer way and see if ye've got the wits to sail through it!"

**Q5 - Long-Term Ambition**

> "Credits come and go, but reputation sticks, matey. One day they'll be tellin' stories about ye in cantinas from here to the Outer Rim. What d'ye want those stories to say?"

## 4. Lead - Scout

**Q1 - Class Development**

> "You can't be good at everything. Anyone who tells you otherwise is either lying or about to become a casualty. So, where do you want to put your effort?"

**Q2 - Career Calling**

> "Training gives you options. It doesn't give you a reason to use them. What sort of work are you actually interested in?"

**Q3 - First Career Situation**

> "All right. Let's put you in that line of work for a moment. Tell me what you'd actually do in this situation, not what you think I want to hear."

**Q4 - Second Career Situation**

> "Different terrain. Different problem. What changes in your approach?"

**Q5 - Long-Term Ambition**

> "People can spend years getting very good at a job they never intended to do. It happens. Where do you actually want to end up?"

## 5. Breach - Soldier

**Q1 - Class Development**

> "Pick what feels right in your hands. Doesn't have to be a cannon, though I recommend one! So, what do you want to get good at?"

**Q2 - Career Calling**

> "Everybody's got their work. What do you want yours to be?"

**Q3 - First Career Situation**

> "All right, say you've got that job, and already something's gone wrong. What do you do?"

**Q4 - Second Career Situation**

> "Now here's a different mess. Bigger guns won't fix every problem, much to my disappointment. What would you try?"

**Q5 - Long-Term Ambition**

> "I know what I want. Keep my people safe, make sure they've got something worth living for. What about you? What do you want to make of yourself?"

## Design controls

1. These lines are fixed per selected starting-class mentor and survey stage. Each fixed line is followed by a separate substantive question and a menu of first-person player-character responses. Dynamic Q2 rankings and Q3/Q4 situations are separate from Mentor dialogue.
2. All five substantive player-answer menus must use first-person character statements, not necessarily the Mentor's speech pattern.
3. No L1 Q6. L1 selections may become evidence for later surveys without becoming achievement, eligibility, or inferred identity automatically.
4. Mechanical traits from automatic grants do not generate preference votes, directly or by tag leakage. Only deliberate player selections produce choice-derived preference evidence.
5. Do not assume old class-owned archetype IDs or scores in existing L1 definitions are canonical V2 parent IDs.
6. Do not expose private Mentor-story reveals through a survey line unless independently authorized.
