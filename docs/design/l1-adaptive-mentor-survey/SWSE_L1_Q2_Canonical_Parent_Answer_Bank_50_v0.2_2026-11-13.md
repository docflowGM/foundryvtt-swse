# SWSE Level 1 Adaptive Mentor Survey - Q2 Canonical Parent Answer Bank (50)

**Local working reference | November 13, 2026 | No Git commit or push**

## Scope and authority

- **Batch 1 (1-25):** User-endorsed example writing from the earlier 25-answer draft, preserved verbatim. Endorsement of narrative wording is not production implementation or mechanical certification.
- **Batch 2 (26-50):** 25 additional proposed first-person career ambitions. Each maps to a **distinct** parent not used in Batch 1.
- All 50 refer to `kind: parent` records in `data/archetypes.json`, verified against the read-only canonical repository blob SHA `76b1ee3aa06dcb29a88ff4ca69bbc8b96cebc3fe` in `docflowGM/foundryvtt-swse`.
- These are a reusable cross-class Q2 answer bank, **not 50 Noble-specific options** and not a replacement for the archetype SSOT.
- Player-facing UI displays **only first-person quotes**; no numbers, career titles, parent IDs, detailed mechanical labels, or scores. The numbered tables below are designer-only.
- Q2 may initially display five personalized career answers plus a no-vote browse/navigation response; all canonical parent careers remain discoverable. Browsing alone confers no intent. Chosen aspirations do not confer skills, talents, features, or eligibility.
- Pending species, class, attributes, and Q1 intent influence dynamic ranking but do not gate unconventional selections. Exact ranking coefficients and A/B slot policy remain open.
