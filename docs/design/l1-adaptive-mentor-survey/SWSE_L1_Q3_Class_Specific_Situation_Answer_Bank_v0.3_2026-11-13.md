## Locked structure and display contract

- Q3 is keyed solely by selected starting foundation class and the exact Q1 development choice; Q2 career has no influence on Q3 scenario selection.
- Exactly **30 scenarios** are retained verbatim from v0.2: five directed choices plus one `Undecided` fallback in each core class.
- Display the approved class Mentor stage introduction separately, then the scenario, then **“What would you do?”**, then five **first-person quoted replies**.
- Player-facing options show **no** scenario IDs, career names, Q1 labels, editorial focuses, ontology tags, numeric scores, or developer metadata.
- Each option offers a plausible method or desired development direction; no implied ownership of special talents, Force powers, feats, trained skills or equipment.
- **Only a selected answer** produces an intent signal. Q1 `Undecided` is neutral, and any unchosen Q3 option is neutral; skipping Q3 records no Q3 intent.
- `editorialFocus` below is an **informal review label only**; it is **not** a new semantic tag ontology, certified signal, or scoring weight. The Q3 answer-to-canonical-ontology and exact-mechanics mappings remain open.
- Q4 remains keyed to an explicitly selected Q2 **parent** and independent of the Q3 answer; the two questions do not branch from each other.

**What would you do?**

- “I would hold the corridor and give the civilians time to escape.”
- “I would draw the raiders away from the trapped people and keep them focused on me.”
