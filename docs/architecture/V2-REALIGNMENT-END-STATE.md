## Draft-only progression
Character creation and level-up write nothing to the actor before final confirmation: the summary no longer stages credits or flags (`chargen-store-no-prefinalization-write`); the world actor is materialized once at the finalization boundary and removed on a definite non-commit (`progression-actor-persistence`). Chargen-time shopping is deferred until the character exists.

## What is NOT claimed
- **Atomicity.** Foundry has no multi-document transaction. Finalization and store settlement are ordered writes with snapshot compensation and an explicit `indeterminate` outcome (see `TE-settlement-guarantees.md`).
- **Runtime behavior.** No `FOUNDRY_TESTED` finding exists; live acceptance is deferred until after merge by owner policy.
- **Unresolved rules.** TPS-RD-001, WEALTH-Q1, DC-A02, DC-A03, DC-A05, PRG-RD-001, PRQ-SRC-001..005, DC-A06-RATE and AUD-RQ-01..31 are open in `docs/audits/swse-rules-decisions-pending.md`; nothing here implements them.
